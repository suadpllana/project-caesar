`/app` is the layout and scrolling half of a document view. A program describes a tree of boxes inside one scrolling view and runs frames. Each frame applies its edits. Then the view is painted. `/app/run_view.py <program>` runs one and prints a line per frame, `<n> <offset> <word>`: the frame's number counting from 1, the scroll offset the frame leaves the view at, and the id of the box the view was held by, or `off scroll`, `off live` or `none`.

The part that keeps the reader's place has been wrong since we rewrote it. Run it on `/app/progs/small.txt`: the first line comes out `1 31 b3`, and it should read `1 31 b4`.

You may change `/app/view/lay.py`, `/app/view/stick.py`, `/app/view/pick.py` and `/app/view/hold.py`, and nothing else. The rest of the tree is replaced by our own copy before anything runs, a new file put beside those four included. The driver is `/app/view/frame.py`. It calls `hold.start` once. Then, for each frame, it calls `hold.before`, applies the edits, and calls `hold.after`, which returns the offset and the word. Every graded program goes through `view.frame.run`, one after another in a single process.

A program is lines of words. `view H` gives the view's height. `box ID PARENT OWN FLAGS` adds a box as the last child of PARENT, or of the document when PARENT is `-`, with its own height OWN; the flags are `pin=T`, `shut`, `lift` and `live`. `at S` gives the offset before the first frame. `frame` starts a frame, and its edits follow, applied in order: `size ID H` sets a box's own height, `add ID PARENT I OWN FLAGS` inserts a new box at index I among all of PARENT's children counting from 0, `drop ID` removes a box and everything in it, `shut ID` and `open ID`, `pin ID T` and `unpin ID`, `lift ID` and `sink ID` set and clear those flags, and `to Y` scrolls the view explicitly to Y. Numbers are non-negative integers, every edit names a box that is in the tree at that moment, and no id is used twice.

The document is laid out from 0 downward in whole rows: a box with top y and height h covers the rows from y up to y + h, not including y + h. A box's height is its own height plus, unless it is shut, the heights of its children that are not lifted, and its children follow its own height in order. A lifted box, the children of a shut box and everything inside either are not laid out and take no space. The scroll range runs from 0 to the document's height less the view's height, or is just 0. Clamping an offset moves it into that range, and every offset a frame prints is clamped into the range of the tree as the frame leaves it. A frame starts from the offset the frame before it printed, or from `at`.

A box flagged `pin=T` is a header with inset T. At offset s a laid-out header of some height is stuck when the lesser of s + T and the end of its section less its height is greater than its top, and it is then drawn with its top there. Its section ends where its parent's box ends, or at the end of the document for a header with no parent. Tops and heights always mean the laid-out ones: where a stuck header is drawn moves nothing. The band at s is how far below s the lowest bottom edge of a stuck header reaches, or 0 when none reaches below s.

Before a frame's edits the view picks the box it is held by, from the tree as it stands, at the offset s it has. The part of the view below the band covers the rows from s plus the band up to s plus the view's height, not including that row, and is empty when the band is as tall as the view; a box shows when it covers some row of that part. Boxes are looked at in document order, starting with the document's children. A box that is lifted, live or of no height, or does not show, is passed over with everything in it. A box whose rows all lie in that part is picked. Any other box is looked into, its children in order by the same rule, unless it is shut, and is picked itself when nothing in it is, so the first box that shows ends the look. The picked box and the boxes containing it, innermost first, are the chain, and each of them has a distance: its top less s less the band, taken before the edits.

After the edits the frame settles in passes, the first beginning at the old offset as it is, clamped or not. A pass reads the band at the offset it begins from and takes the first box of the chain that still qualifies there: in the tree, laid out, of some height, and neither stuck nor inside a box that is. If none does, the frame prints `none` at the offset the pass began from. Otherwise the pass asks for that box's top, less its own distance, less the band, clamped. Asking for the offset it began from ends the frame, held by that box; otherwise the next pass begins at the offset asked for. After four passes that all moved, the frame takes the smallest offset any of them asked for, held by the box of the first pass that asked for it. A view at offset 0 is held like any other.

Two things switch holding off, and they come before everything above. An explicit scroll switches it off, and the frame prints `off scroll` at the offset of its last `to`. A frame with an edit naming a box that is live or inside a live box, for `add` the new box, prints `off live` at its old offset. A frame with both prints `off scroll`. When neither happened and nothing was picked before the edits, the frame prints `none` at its old offset.
