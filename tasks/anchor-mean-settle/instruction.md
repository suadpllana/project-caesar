`/app` is the list panel out of our client, cut down to the part that decides layout. The panel shows a view 300 tall onto a list of rows; a row is a run of text whose height follows from how long it is and how wide the panel is, and it gets measured only when a render pass reaches it. Until then it is assumed to be as tall as the mean, rounded down, of the heights of the rows that have been measured, taken over the rows still in the list, and 24 tall while none of them has been. Programs are plain text, one op to a line, and four of them sit in `/app/progs`. `bulk n lo sp` is the import shorthand, appending `n` rows named `k1` onwards and carrying on across later bulks, the i-th of them counting from zero carrying `lo + (i mod sp)` characters. `ins i id ln` puts a new row of `ln` characters at index `i`, `del id` takes a row out, `move id i` takes one out and puts it back at index `i` of what is left, `set id ln` gives a row different text, `span w` re-widths the panel, `roll d` scrolls by `d`, and `pass` is a render pass. Of the three questions, `top` asks where the view sits, `tall` how tall the list is, and `face` which row the view touches first. Names are unique, an index is inside the list, and naming a row that is not in the list does nothing at all. `/app/run_pan.py` runs a program and prints a line wherever the panel has something to report.

Six files may change and no others - `/app/pan/grid.py`, `/app/pan/gues.py`, `/app/pan/seat.py`, `/app/pan/step.py`, `/app/pan/edit.py`, `/app/pan/ask.py`.

How tall the list is counts a measured row at the height it was measured at and every other row at the assumed height. The scroll position runs from 0 up to that figure less the height of the view, and stays at 0 while the list is shorter than the view; every landing is clamped into that range.

The view is held against one row. That row is the first the view touches, and what is remembered alongside it is the distance from the view's top edge down to the row's own, which is zero when the two line up and negative when the row began above the view. Scrolling clamps and then takes whichever row the view has come to touch. A pass takes one before it measures anything, and goes on holding it afterwards. Anything that changes the list puts the scroll position back where that row sits at that distance, so a row arriving ahead of it moves the position by as much as it moved the row and what is on screen stands still. The distance is never recomputed to absorb a clamp: at either end of the scroll range part of the correction has nowhere to go, and what is lost there is lost.

A pass measures the rows the view touches, one at a time and lowest first, putting the scroll position back after each one and asking again what the view touches, until nothing the view touches is unmeasured. It reports how many rows it measured.

Deleting the row the view is held against leaves the view held against whichever row took its index, or against the last row when the deleted one was last, at the same distance as before; deleting the last row of all leaves the view holding on to nothing and the position at 0. A move carries the row's measurement with it, and carries the view with it when the view was held against that row. Different text costs a row its measurement, and with it the mean loses a sample. Re-widthing the panel gives up every measurement it has.

`face` names the first row the view touches together with that row's edge relative to the view's, and reads `face none` when there are no rows at all.

Lines go out as they happen, and there are four of them: a pass gives `seen <rows measured>`, and the questions give `top <position>`, `tall <height>`, and `face <row> <edge>` or `face none`. The panel prints nothing besides these.

`/app/progs/wide.txt` imports a hundred and twenty thousand rows and rolls and passes across them six thousand times, and `/app/progs/deep.txt` imports a hundred and fifty thousand, measures the band at the far end, then edits at the front nine thousand times.
