`/app` is the bind stage of a build tool, cut down to the part that decides what goes into an image. A unit carries parts, and a part has a size in bytes, at most one claim key, the names it gives and the names it uses, each of those either strongly or weakly; a unit may also spare a name at a size, which is not a part and gives nothing. A bundle is an ordered list of units that is scanned on demand, not loaded. A program is a text file of ops and `/app/progs` holds four of them. `u <unit>` opens a unit and `p <size> <key>` adds a part to it, numbered from 0 in the order they are added, with `-` for no key, `g <name> s|w` and `r <name> s|w` say what that part gives and uses, and `t <name> <size>` spares a name for the unit. `b <bundle> <unit> ...` declares a bundle over units already declared, and `bulk <bundle> chain <n> <step>` is the import shorthand that synthesises `n` members, the i-th giving `<bundle>x<i>` from a first part that uses `<bundle>x<i+step>` where there is one, and `<bundle>y<i>` from a second part under key `<bundle>k<i mod 256>`. `root <name>` and `hold <unit> <part>` say where the image is reached from, `link` takes the input list, whose items are units, bundles, or bundles wrapped in a bracket pair, and of the two questions `at <name>` asks where a name stands while `img` asks what the image holds. `/app/run_bind.py` takes a program and prints a line for each thing that happens.

The last line comes out `at f wk 0` and it should read `at f st 0`, since a weak give does not settle a name and the bundle holds a member that gives it strongly. The files you may change are `/app/bind/hold.py`, `/app/bind/want.py`, `/app/bind/pull.py`, `/app/bind/place.py`, `/app/bind/prune.py` and `/app/bind/wire.py`. Nothing else.

Loading a unit walks its parts in order, and a part whose claim key is already held is dropped, so what it would have given and what it would have used never enter. A part of a unit named on the input list is kept even where its key is held, provided the part holding it came out of a bundle: that part is dropped instead, and what it gave and used leaves before the arriving part enters. A key held by a part of a unit named on the input list never changes hands, and a member taken out of a bundle never takes one.

A name is wanted while some kept part uses it strongly, or some loaded unit spares it, and no kept part gives it strongly. A weak give settles nothing, so a name that has only those stays wanted, and a weak use wants nothing at all.

A bundle is scanned from its first member. The first member that strongly gives a wanted name is taken and loaded, and the scan then starts again at the first member; the bundle is finished when a pass over it takes nothing. Bundles inside a bracket pair are scanned one after another, and the pair again from its first bundle while a pass over it takes anything. The input list itself is walked once. A unit is loaded once whatever names it afterwards, a member already loaded is passed over, not taken, and a take is never unsaid: the member stays in and its parts stay kept even where what pulled it has since left.

The strong gives of a name stand in the order they arrived and the name stands on the first of them. One arriving where there is already one prints and changes nothing. When the first leaves, the name stands on the next, and nothing is printed for that. A name that has no strong give stands on the first weak give it collected.

A name that no kept part gives and that some unit spared is placed, at the largest size spared for it, against the first unit in load order that spared it at that size.

When the input list is finished the image is what the roots reach. A root name reaches whatever it stands on, a held part is reached outright where it is in at all, and from a reached part every name it uses strongly is reached while every name it uses weakly is not; a placed name is reached the way a part is. Nothing else is in the image, and a name whose part is not in the image stands on nothing.

Every line is printed when it happens. A member taken prints `take <bundle> <unit>` and a second strong give prints `dup <name> <unit>`, while `at <name> <unit> <part>` answers where a name stands, `at <name> spare <unit> <size>` answers where it was placed and `at <name> none` answers that it stands on nothing, and `img <parts> <bytes>` counts what the image holds and adds up the sizes. Nothing else is printed.

`/app/progs/wide.txt` synthesises a bundle of sixty thousand members and takes every other one, and `/app/progs/deep.txt` synthesises the same bundle and takes all of them.
