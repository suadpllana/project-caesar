`/app` is the planner our batch platform runs when a vendor restates one partition of a source dataset. `/app/run_plan.py` takes a pipeline file and prints the plan that brings the platform back into agreement with the corrected source, and `/app/pipes` holds three pipeline files.

Hours are counted from hour 0, where the history starts. Hourly partition h spans hours h to h+1, daily partition d spans hours 24d to 24d+24, and a partition ends where its span ends. A pipeline file has one declaration per line. `now t` sets the current hour. `src n g k` declares a source dataset and `step n g k r...` a dataset a step computes from its reads r, where g is the grain, `h` or `d`, and k is the keep in hours. A step reads only datasets declared above it, apart from its own previous partition. A read takes one of four forms. Plain `x` reads the same partition of x, which has the same grain as the step, and `x~w` reads the w partitions of such an x ending with the one of the same number. In a daily step, `x/d` reads the 24 hours of its day from the hourly x. The last form, `x-1`, reads the latest partition of x that ends at or before the partition of the step starts: the day before, for an hourly step reading a daily x, and the last hour of the day before, for a daily step reading an hourly x. A read that falls before hour 0 reads nothing there. `stand r x` says that the daily r reads only `x/d`, `pin n p...` names partitions of n that were published, and `fix n p` names the corrected partition, which belongs to a source and exists.

A partition exists from the hour it ends until its keep runs out, while end <= now < end + keep, so an hourly partition 10 with a keep of 20 exists from hour 11 to hour 30 and is gone at hour 31. A published partition exists from the hour it ends, whatever its keep, and it is never rewritten. The correction reaches the corrected partition, and every partition of a step that has ended by now and whose reads name a partition the correction reaches, whether or not the partitions in between still exist.

Every reached partition of a step that exists gets exactly one line. A published one is held as `pinned`. Any other is held as `lost` when it cannot be computed, otherwise held as `same` when nothing it reads has changed, and otherwise rerun.

Computing a partition, for a rerun or for the plan, reads what its step declares. A partition that exists is read as the plan leaves it. One that does not is computed for the plan from its own reads in the same way, once however many computations read it, and it is not kept. A source partition that does not exist cannot be computed, and neither can anything that has to read one. One exception covers a roll-up declared by `stand r x`: when any computation except those of r itself reads `x/d` for a day and any hour of that day does not exist, it reads the partition of r for that day in place of all 24 hours, provided that partition exists and agrees. Otherwise it reads the hours.

Two things are known of every partition a computation reads: whether it has changed, and whether it agrees with the corrected source. The corrected partition has changed and agrees. A partition the correction does not reach has not changed and agrees, while a held partition has not changed and does not agree. A rerun has changed. So has a partition computed for the plan when anything it read has changed, and either one agrees when everything it read agrees.

A rerun prints `run <dataset> <partition> <mode>`. The mode is `part` when anything it read does not agree, otherwise `sub` when it read a roll-up in place of hours, and otherwise `full`. A partition computed for a rerun prints `temp <dataset> <partition>`, and so does one computed for a partition printed that way; one computed only for a held partition prints nothing. Each hold is printed as `hold <dataset> <partition> <reason>`, and partitions are printed by number.

The runs and temps come first. Each follows the lines of everything it read: the rerun of a partition it read, a partition computed for it, and the rerun of a roll-up it read in place of hours. Of the lines free to go next, the one whose partition ends first goes, and of two that end together, the one whose dataset is declared first. The holds follow, by the hour they end and then by declaration. Nothing else is printed.
