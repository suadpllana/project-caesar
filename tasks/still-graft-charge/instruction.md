`/app` is the space ledger of the store, cut down to the part that decides what a frozen image is keeping alive and which line is charged for it. A line is a mutable image: a map from cell to block. A still is a line frozen. A graft is a line started from a still. A program is a text file of ops, and `/app/progs` holds four of them. `line L` starts an empty line, `put L lo hi z` lays a fresh block of size `z` on every cell of the range, `cut L lo hi` takes that range out of the head, `still L n` takes still `n` on line `L`, `graft n L` starts line `L` from still `n`, `lift L` lifts a grafted line, `drop n` drops a still, `cap L z` puts a ceiling on a line, and of the two questions `ask L` asks what a line is charged while `at L k` asks which put laid the block its head holds at cell `k`. A range names every cell between its ends, both included. Every name a program uses was made by an earlier op and no name is made twice, and no still is named again once it has been dropped. `/app/run_log.py` takes a program and prints a line for each thing that happens.

We rewrote this half last cycle and it has been wrong since. Run `/app/run_log.py` on `/app/progs/tiny.txt`. The last line comes out `charge p 0`. It should read `charge p 8`: the line holds one block, and a still it owns holding that block too does not make it shared. The files you may change are `/app/led/cell.py`, `/app/led/hold.py`, `/app/led/cost.py`, `/app/led/tree.py`, `/app/led/gate.py` and `/app/led/free.py`. Nothing else.

A block is what a put lays, one per cell of its range, each of the size that put names. Every put that is taken takes the next number, the first taking 1. Every block it lays carries that number. A put over a cell the head already holds a block at leaves the head holding only the new one. A cut leaves the head holding nothing at the cells it names. Neither of them settles anything else about the old blocks. What becomes of a block is decided by who holds it.

A line holds a block when its head holds it, or when a still the line owns holds it. A still holds what the head of its line held at the moment it was taken. Nothing that happens afterwards changes what a still holds. The head of a graft starts out holding exactly what its origin still holds, and stops holding one of those cells when the graft writes over that cell or cuts it. The charge of a line is the total size of the blocks that line holds and no other line holds.

A lift changes a graft and the line it was grafted from around. The stills of that line up to and including the origin, in the order they were taken, become the stills of the graft and stand before the ones it already had; the graft is then grafted from whatever the line above was grafted from, or from nothing if that line was grafted from nothing; and the line above becomes a graft of the origin still. A lift of a line that was not grafted does nothing. A still that a lift has moved goes on holding what it held when it was taken. The line holding those blocks is the line that owns the still now.

A still that a line was grafted from cannot be dropped: `drop n` prints `busy n` and changes nothing. Any other drop takes the still away, and prints `free n z`, where `z` is the total size of the blocks that still was holding which no line holds any more.

A cap is a ceiling on the charge of a line. A put on a capped line is taken only if the charge that line would be left with, once the put had landed, is at most the cap. Otherwise the put prints `full L`, lays no block, takes no number, and leaves the line as it was. A line with no cap takes every put. `cap L z` replaces any ceiling the line already had. A put is measured against the cap of the line it is on and against no other.

`ask L` prints `charge L z` with the charge of that line. `at L k` prints `at L k` and the number of the put that laid the block the head holds at that cell, or `at L k -` when the head holds nothing there. A put prints only when it is refused. A line, a cap, a cut, a still, a graft and a lift print nothing at all. The lines come out in the order the ops run, one per line, on standard output.
