`/app` grades recorded agent runs against a milestone rubric. A trail file is one session. It gives the records every episode in it shares, then the episodes, each with its own rubric and the steps its run was cut into. `/app/run_crd.py` takes one and prints a line for each thing that happens. `cfg B` says how many actions an episode may perform; `rec K V` gives record K the value V before the first episode; `ep NAME` begins an episode. `goal G W N P... KIND ARGS` adds goal G of weight W standing on the N goals P. Its predicate is `at K V` (record K has exactly the value V), `up K V` (record K has a value of at least V) or `off K` (record K has no value at all, and a record standing at zero has one). `bar B G KIND K` adds violation B, which names goal G and watches record K for `lost`, `gain` or `back`. `step` begins a step. `put K V` and `cut K` are the actions inside it, and `ok` or `err` ends it. Goals and violations are numbered from zero inside each episode. A goal only stands on goals before it. `/app/trails` holds four trail files.

The rest of the tree is replaced by our own copy before a trail is run, a new file put beside those seven included. Our copy of `/app/run_crd.py` parses the trail, makes one `crd.store.Store` and seeds it with `seed(key, value)`, makes one `crd.tally.Sum`, calls `crd.ep.run(cfg, episode, store, sums, out)` for each episode in file order, then `crd.tally.close(sums, out)`, and prints the lines `out` collected.

An observation happens at the end of a step that ended `ok`. One more happens as an episode opens, and that one credits nothing and fires nothing: it only records the values the next observation is compared against. A step that ended `err` leaves the records exactly as it found them and observes nothing, so what it wrote is never seen.

At an observation the goals are gone over once, in ascending order. A goal that is not credited is credited when its predicate holds and every goal it stands on was credited at a strictly earlier observation of this episode. Credit then stays. A goal keeps it when its predicate stops holding, and is never credited a second time.

After that the violations are gone over in ascending order. `lost K` holds when record K had a value at the previous observation of this episode and has none now. `gain K` holds when it had none then and has one now. `back K` holds when it had a value at both and the value now is smaller than the one it had. A violation that holds fires. Firing shuts the goal it names, whether or not that goal is credited, and takes the credit of that goal and of every goal standing on it, directly or through others. Of the goals a firing touches, only the one the violation names is shut. A shut goal is opened again at the first later observation at which its predicate does not hold, and cannot be credited before that.

Every `put` and `cut` counts against the episode's budget, the ones in a step that ended `err` included. An episode may perform at most B actions. The action after that is not performed, the rest of its step is not performed either, that step is rolled back and the episode is closed there. An episode closed that way has its records put back to what they were as it opened. One that ran to the end of its steps leaves its records as they stand. Either way the credit it earned stands. The next episode opens on whatever records are left. Each episode starts with its own budget, nothing credited and nothing shut.

`mark G` is printed when goal G is credited and `void G` when it loses its credit, each in ascending goal order within an observation. `fire B` comes before the voids that violation B caused. When an episode is over it prints `ep NAME CREDIT WHOLE USED`. CREDIT adds up the weights of the goals it holds, WHOLE adds up the weights of every goal of its rubric, and USED is the number of actions charged to it. When the trail is over it prints `run EPISODES FULL CREDIT WHOLE`. FULL counts the episodes holding every goal of their rubric, and the last two add up over the episodes. Nothing else is printed.
